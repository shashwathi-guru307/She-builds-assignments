FIG. 01 — ABOUT

The person behind the code.

I'm Shashwathi, a third-year B.Tech Information Technology student based in Tamil Nadu, India, working toward a placement at a company where I can keep building real things — not just pass exams about them.

My interests sit at the intersection of AI and cybersecurity, and I care more about understanding how a system actually works than memorizing how to describe it. That's why most of what I learn ends up as a working project: an Android app, a recovery tracker, or right now, an AI assistant that runs entirely offline on Windows.

I'm an INFJ who likes structure — clear phases, clean architecture diagrams, organized folders — but I bring a creative eye to how things look and feel, not just how they function. Alongside my technical work, I'm actively practicing English communication and public speaking, because I know shipping the project is only half the job.
